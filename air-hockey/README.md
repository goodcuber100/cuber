# Puck Rush

Air hockey in one HTML file, with no build step and no install. Open `index.html` in a browser, or host the folder on any static host.

The table turns to fit the screen: horizontal on a wide screen, vertical on a phone held upright. To lock either orientation, use the **Table** option in the menu or the pause screen.

## Modes

**Classic (real time).** Your mallet follows your mouse or finger, with no clicking or grabbing. It stays in your half, and it moves at a capped speed so a fast flick hits hard without passing through the puck. Keyboard players use `WASD`, and in a 2-player game player 2 uses the arrow keys. With 2 players on a touch screen, each person touches their own half.

**Play by Play (slingshot).** Players take turns. Tap anywhere in your half to move your mallet there. Then grab the mallet, pull it back and let go: the farther you pull, the harder it flies. While you shoot, the other player's mallet waits in front of its goal as a blocker, so you often need a bank shot off the side rail. Hit a power-up with the **puck** to collect it:

| Power-up | Effect |
|---|---|
| Turbo | Your next launch hits 50% harder |
| Giant | Your mallet grows for your next two shots |
| Shield | A wall covers your goal until it blocks one shot |
| Double | Your next goal counts twice |
| Extra shot | You shoot again after this turn |
| Ghost | Your next shot passes straight through the other mallet |

## Options

- **Opponent:** Easy, Medium or Hard CPU, or 2 players on one device.
- **Goals to win:** 3, 5, 7 or 10.
- **Table:** fit to screen, horizontal or vertical.
- **Sound:** on or off. Sounds are synthesized, so there are no audio files.

`Esc` or `P` pauses the game.
